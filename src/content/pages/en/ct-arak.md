## Reporting and priority information

Our radiology exams are always reported by our specialists, with a full analysis of the images and professional review.

**Our standard report turnaround time: 4 working days**

When reporting, we pay particular attention to a detailed professional assessment and proper quality assurance, so exams are processed according to a structured priority system. In certain clinical situations – for example urgent surgical, oncology or acute musculoskeletal cases – you can request priority reporting, subject to limited capacity. If you have questions, our staff will gladly tell you about the available options [**by phone**](/en/contact) or in person.
