---
title: Booking deposit for special exams
lead: Please follow the rules below when you arrive.
summary: |-
  Please note that for the exams below, we can only give you an appointment once you have paid a booking deposit in advance:

  - Abdomen and pelvis exams and all exams with contrast: **booking deposit: 50% of the exam fee**
  - Exams of 2 or more regions (e.g. both ankles, both knees, cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine exams, etc.): **booking deposit: HUF 30,000**
---

After you book, we **send you a payment request** for the booking deposit. Please **pay the amount by bank transfer** within 24 hours to the account number shown on the payment request.

If the booking deposit is **not paid within 24 hours of booking**, the appointment is canceled automatically and the exam will not take place.

After the exam, we **issue an invoice for the full exam fee**, with the booking deposit credited against it.

For these types of exams, **you can cancel an appointment free of charge** only **up to 48 hours before the exam**. After that, our health center invoices the booking deposit in the patient's name as an availability fee, based on the details given at booking, and it is not refunded.

Please also note that if, at booking, **we give you an appointment less than 48 hours before the exam**, an appointment for a multi-region or contrast exam cannot be canceled free of charge.

If you do not attend or cancel late, the booking deposit is kept as an availability fee and is not refunded.
