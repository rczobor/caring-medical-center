### When is the exam worth having, and what conditions can the pain be linked to?

A spine MRI reveals changes in the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine, such as a herniated disc, narrowing of the spinal canal, a collapsed vertebra (from injury or osteoporosis), arthrosis of the joints, inflammation of the discs or vertebrae, diseases of the spinal cord and spinal nerves, tumors and other changes.

### When is a spine MRI scan recommended?

- A bulging intervertebral disc
- Lower back pain
- Pain radiating into the arms or legs
- Neck pain
- Headache, dizziness
- Wear of the intervertebral discs
- Narrowing of the spinal canal
- Instability or slipping of a vertebra
- Wear of the vertebral joints (osteoarthritis)
- Bone damage, up to a fracture
- Injury to the spinal cord, the spinal nerves or the soft tissues
- Metastases and/or tumors of the bony spine, the spinal cord or spinal nerves, the spinal canal or the soft tissues
- Inflammation of the vertebral bodies, the intervertebral discs and the spinal cord (such as multiple sclerosis or transverse myelitis)
