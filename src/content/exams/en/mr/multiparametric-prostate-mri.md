## Multiparametric prostate MRI scan

A prostate MRI is not suitable on its own as a prostate cancer screening test; other additional exams are also needed.

A prostate MRI scan helps determine the position of changes within the prostate tissue. It can also help detect tumors and estimate the risks involved.

Prostate diseases, tumors, urination problems and an enlarged prostate are common problems in men.

Compared with a physical (rectal) exam and ultrasound, a multiparametric MRI of the prostate can clarify at an early stage why your PSA level is outside the normal range.

A multiparametric MRI scan shows changes in the prostate tissue and can tell them apart, so benign, malignant and inflammatory prostate diseases can usually be distinguished even at an early stage. With an optimized mpMRI of the prostate, 50% of patients can be spared a biopsy, because a biopsy is only needed if cancer is suspected. A biopsy (taking a tissue sample) then clarifies how aggressive the cancer is and which treatment suits the patient.

### Which symptoms call for a prostate MRI scan?

- Frequent urge to urinate or problems holding urine
- Difficulty urinating
- Bleeding
