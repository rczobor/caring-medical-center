## Orbit MRI (eye socket) scan

An orbit MRI scan is a thorough exam of the eye socket. It is used to detect changes there, damage to the optic nerves, inflammation of the optic nerve or the eye muscles, and possible tumors.

The exam is recommended when pain occurs around or in the eye, when vision problems appear, or after a consultation with a specialist.
