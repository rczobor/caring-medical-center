## Sacrum MRI (sacroiliac joint) scan

We mainly do a sacrum MRI scan for inflammatory diseases of the sacroiliac joints (where the pelvis meets the sacrum).

### Which symptoms call for a sacrum MRI scan?

- Pain around the pelvis after an injury or accident
- Autoimmune joint inflammation
- Severe lower back pain that affects the legs and walking
