MRI is an excellent way to examine the knee joint and its soft tissues, including different parts of the knee such as the cartilage (meniscus), the knee ligament (ACL) and the kneecap (patella).

### When is a knee MRI scan worth having?

- joint inflammation, cartilage detachment, torn ligament (ACL), sports injury (sprain, blow)
