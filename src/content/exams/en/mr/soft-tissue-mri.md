## Soft tissue MRI (thigh, calf, foot, forearm, upper arm, hand)

MRI makes it possible to examine the soft tissues (connective tissue), muscles, tendons and blood vessels of the arms and legs.

### When is the exam recommended?

- Torn or strained muscle
- Hematoma (bruising)
- Edema
- A lump under the skin in the soft tissue
- Tumor or metastasis
