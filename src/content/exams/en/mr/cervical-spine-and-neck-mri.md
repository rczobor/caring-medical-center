## Cervical spine and neck soft tissue MRI scan

MRI can diagnose various diseases and changes of the cervical spine. It gives useful information about scars after surgery and about the condition of the vertebrae. It is most often used to examine the condition of the discs. It is excellent for identifying possible herniations and developmental or injury-related abnormalities.

### When is a cervical spine exam recommended?

- Long-standing numbness in the arms
- Neck pain
- Pain radiating into the shoulder
- Arm numbness
- Headache
