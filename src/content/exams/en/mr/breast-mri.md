It is very important to know that it does not replace a complete mammography workup, but it works very well as an additional exam when one is needed. For the first exam, mammography and ultrasound are the right methods.

### What are the advantages of a breast MRI scan?

- no X-ray radiation
- no compression of the chest
- works well for small breasts
- especially suitable for women with very dense glandular tissue
- for checking breast implants
- for follow-up and for clarifying a recurrence of known breast cancer

### What conditions can the pain or symptom be linked to?

A breast MRI also gives certainty about any damage to breast implants, their position and its extent. Breast cancer is the most common type of cancer in women: one in eight women develops it. The biggest risk factors are age, a family history of the disease and genetic factors. The aim of breast exams is to find breast cancer at the earliest possible stage, when the chance of a cure is best.
