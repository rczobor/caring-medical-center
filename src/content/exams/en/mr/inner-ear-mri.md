An MRI scan can help diagnose changes in the inner ear, as well as tumors of the cerebellum, cysts and other changes. Its main use is to investigate sensorineural (nerve-related) hearing loss.

### When is an inner ear MRI scan worth having?

- Worsening hearing
- Dizziness
- Headache

If you have these symptoms, we also recommend a consultation with a specialist.
