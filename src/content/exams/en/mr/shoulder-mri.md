## Shoulder joint MRI (arthrography)

Shoulder injuries (from accidents) and wear are very well examined with a shoulder joint MRI.

### When is the exam worth having?

- Shoulder injury
- Joint inflammation
- Rotator cuff tear
- Cartilage detachment
- Frozen shoulder
