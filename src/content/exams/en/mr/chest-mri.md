A chest MRI is used to diagnose the blood vessels, shape and function of the heart. It shows changes in the heart and the surrounding region very well. It is especially recommended for diagnosing chest wall abnormalities and possible signs of a tumor, and for mapping lung tumors that affect the esophagus.

### When, and for which symptoms, is the exam recommended?

It is very well suited to examining the intercostal muscles, breastbone pain and the soft tissues around them, but not the lung tissue itself.
