## Hip MRI – arthrography

MRI allows a detailed exam of the hip joint. It can identify inflammation and tumors around the hip and the pain that comes with them. The exam can be especially useful if an X-ray does not show any bone changes.

### When is a hip MRI scan recommended?

- Long-standing inflammation or pain
- Joint wear (osteoarthritis)
