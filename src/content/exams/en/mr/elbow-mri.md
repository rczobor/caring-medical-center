An elbow MRI shows the condition of the periosteum (the membrane covering the bone) and changes in the cartilage and tendons. It gives a clear picture of the bursae (fluid-filled sacs) and the muscles.

### Which symptoms call for an elbow MRI scan?

- Torn ligament, sports injury, joint inflammation
- Tennis elbow, fluid and edema, rheumatic-type pain
