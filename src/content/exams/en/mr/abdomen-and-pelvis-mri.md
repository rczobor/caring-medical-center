## Abdomen and pelvis MRI scan

### Do you have digestive problems or complaints? When is the exam worth having?

If you suffer from abdominal cramps, pain or bloating, unpleasant abdominal cramps, abdominal pain, possibly bowel problems, unpleasant gas, or pain in the lower back or abdomen, an MRI can be the way to the right diagnosis.

MRI is strong at clarifying the abdomen, the abdominal soft tissues (muscles, blood vessels, fat tissue, connective tissue) and all organs of the pelvis and the retroperitoneum. The advantage of MRI over all other imaging methods is its especially wide range of options for telling tissues apart, together with the high image quality that modern equipment can achieve.

### MRCP, an exam of the bile ducts:

This exam looks at the bile ducts and the pancreatic duct. It is used to detect diseases and blockages of the bile ducts, gallstones, and narrowing of the gallbladder or bile ducts.
