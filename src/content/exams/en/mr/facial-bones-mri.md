## Head MRI, head MR angiography, facial bones MRI

### Which symptoms call for a head MRI scan? What conditions can they be a sign of?

Conditions and the anatomy of the head and brain are diagnosed most precisely with an MRI scan. A head MRI scan is recommended for the following problems:

- vision problems
- headache
- dizziness, loss of balance
- symptoms of multiple sclerosis
- prevention
- Circulatory disorders, such as signs of narrowed blood vessels
- Degenerative diseases of the brain (such as Alzheimer's disease)
- metabolic disorders, such as diabetes
- abnormal widening of a blood vessel (aneurysm)
- Inflammatory diseases of the meninges (meningitis)
- After an injury to the head
- Investigating developmental or brain abnormalities
- epilepsy
- Locating a tumor
