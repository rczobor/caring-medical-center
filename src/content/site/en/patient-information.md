## Do you need a referral?

- **MRI scan:** No, you don't need a referral. You can book an MRI or another diagnostic exam with us directly. An exam with contrast can only be done with a specialist's recommendation and a prior kidney function lab result.
- **Ultrasound:** can be done even without a referral.
- **Lab tests:** the most common blood, urine and hormone tests can be done without a referral.
- **Accident injury:** a trauma specialist sees you first and makes the recommendation for an MRI scan. Details are on the [trauma surgery](/en/specialist-clinics/urgent-care) page.

If you're not sure which exam you need, [call our clinic](/en/contact) or see [how we can help](/en#panaszok).

## How do I prepare?

- **MRI scan:** arrive 10–15 minutes before your appointment to fill in the registration form. If you have a pacemaker, an aneurysm clip, a drug infusion pump or an implant, you must always check with our staff whether the exam can be done. If you are pregnant, the exam can only be done at a specialist's request. You'll find the details, including what to know about claustrophobia, on the [MRI scans](/en/mri) page.
- **X-ray:** some X-ray exams require you to arrive on an empty stomach, for example a kidney X-ray without contrast.
- **Lab tests:** before certain lab tests, such as a blood sugar test, you need to arrive on an empty stomach. Otherwise you can eat and drink before the test and take your usual medication.

## Exams with contrast

An exam with contrast can only be done with a specialist's recommendation and a prior kidney function lab result. We can only give you an appointment once you have paid a booking deposit in advance: it is 50% of the exam fee.

## What should I bring?

- your ID card and TAJ card (Hungarian social security card),
- earlier reports, hospital discharge summaries and images related to your complaint,
- for an MRI scan with contrast, the specialist's recommendation and your kidney function lab result,
- a list of the medicines you take regularly.

## When will I get my report?

After an MRI scan, you get your images on a CD, and we send the specialist's evaluation by email. Our standard report turnaround time is 4 working days.

If you already have a report and would like a second specialist opinion, ask for a [radiology second opinion](/en/medreport).

## Payment and cancellation

The prices of our exams and treatments are on our [price lists](/en/prices). You can also pay through a health fund; we list our partners on the [prices page](/en/prices).

For abdominal and pelvic exams, exams with contrast and multi-region exams, we can only give you an appointment once you have paid a booking deposit in advance. You can read the terms for paying it and for cancelling free of charge on the [contact page](/en/contact#foglalasi-dij).
