_Comprehensive assessment and targeted therapy in one place_

Spinal pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal complaints today, and it has a major impact on your ability to work, your sleep and your quality of life. At the Caring Medical Point spine care clinic, **assessment, diagnosis and treatment take place in one unified system, with modern facilities behind them** – quickly, in an organized way and with the patient at the center.

## Modern diagnostics on site: X-ray and MRI

Accurately assessing spinal conditions is unthinkable without modern imaging. One of the biggest advantages of Caring Medical Point is that **X-ray and MRI are available on site**, so your assessment doesn't move between several institutions but is part of one coordinated care process.

A digital X-ray quickly shows the bony structures, the alignment of the spine, and any degenerative changes or instability. An MRI scan shows the intervertebral discs, the nerve structures, the state of the spinal canal and the soft tissues in detail. This is especially important for a herniated disc, nerve root involvement, or stubborn, recurring pain.

**On-site diagnostics mean the specialist's decision can be data-driven, accurate and made without losing time.**

## When should you see a spine specialist?

A specialist consultation is recommended if:

- you have long-lasting lower back, back or neck pain
- the pain radiates into your arm or leg
- you have numbness, muscle weakness or altered sensation
- you have a known herniated disc or scoliosis
- you need a post-operative check-up or assessment

The exam starts with a detailed medical history, followed by a physical examination that assesses your range of motion, nerve function and muscle balance.

## The role of physiotherapy: the key to lasting improvement

Many spine problems can be treated conservatively. A key role here is played by our **experienced on-site physiotherapy team**, who put together targeted, personalized exercise therapy based on the specialist's diagnosis.

Because the spine specialist and the physiotherapist work together in one place, treatment is much more effective. The diagnosis, the treatment plan and its delivery all follow one shared professional approach.

## A personalized treatment plan

Treatment is always individual. In milder cases, targeted physiotherapy and lifestyle advice may be enough. If needed, medication, injection therapy or a further specialist consultation may also be considered.

The goal is always for you to regain your mobility, have less pain and reach a stable condition in the long term.
