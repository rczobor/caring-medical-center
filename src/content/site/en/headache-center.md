## Online headache consultation – a modern and safe way to an accurate diagnosis

Headache is a symptom that significantly affects everyday life: many people suffer from it, yet an accurate diagnosis and the right treatment are often delayed. The online headache consultation aims to offer a fast, accessible, high-standard solution for everyone looking for personalized help.

In an online consultation, you join the visit comfortably from home and receive the same professional attention and care as if you came in person.

## How does the consultation work?

Online visit (by video call): a detailed medical history, a discussion of what your headache is like and how often it happens, and a review of any reports you have (MRI, CT, lab).

## Professional assessment and diagnosis

An accurate diagnosis based on international clinical guidelines.

## Personalized treatment plan

Treatment for attacks, preventive medication and lifestyle advice, and setting up a headache diary.

## Written summary

After every consultation, you receive a detailed document about what was discussed.

## Who is the online headache consultation for?

- People with recurring headaches or a new type of headache.
- Migraine patients who would like personalized, up-to-date therapy.
- People who would like to discuss their MRI or CT report in relation to their headache.
- People who need a fast, accessible specialist opinion – without a waiting list.
- Patients looking for long-term care and modern prevention strategies.

## What are the benefits of the service?

- Fast access to a specialist, without a long waiting list.
- A secure online system that complies with Hungarian law and data protection rules.
- A personalized treatment plan, not one-size-fits-all solutions.
- Complete documentation – every visit is also uploaded to EESZT, the Hungarian national e-health system.

## Up-to-date treatment options

Ongoing follow-up: headache diary, follow-up consultations, long-term management.

## Why choose this service?

The methods and treatment recommendations used in the consultation are based on the latest international clinical recommendations.

You can be confident that you receive comprehensive, modern and safe care – all online, in a convenient way.
