If your exam takes place before 08:00 or after 20:00, an on-call fee of HUF 5,000 is charged on top of the exam fee. Urgent exams done during opening hours without a booked appointment, and urgent exams on weekends outside opening hours, cost HUF 69,900 per region. Reporting and priority information. All our radiology exams are reported by our specialists, with full image analysis and professional review.

Our standard report turnaround time is 4 working days. When reporting, we pay particular attention to detailed professional evaluation and proper quality assurance, so exams are processed under a structured priority system. In certain clinical situations – for example urgent surgical, oncology or acute musculoskeletal cases – you can request priority reporting, subject to limited capacity. If you have questions, our staff will gladly tell you about the available options [by phone](/en/contact) or in person.

The prices shown apply uniformly, regardless of region, to MRI scans both without contrast and with contrast. The areas that each count as one region are: brain, facial bones, cervical spine, thoracic spine, lumbar spine, sacroiliac joint, hip, shoulder, elbow, wrist, hand, knee, ankle, foot, thigh, upper arm, forearm, chest, abdomen and pelvis. If more than one region needs to be examined, the price is multiplied by the number of regions examined. An exam with contrast can only be done with a specialist's recommendation and a prior kidney function lab result.
