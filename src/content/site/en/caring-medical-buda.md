**Caring Medical** Buda is a **private diagnostic and imaging healthcare provider in Hungary**, offering modern diagnostic technology, fast booking and professional patient care at its Budapest center.

## Services

Caring Medical Buda mainly carries out diagnostic exams, including:

## Imaging diagnostics

- [**MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) scans**](/en/mri) – radiation-free, detailed imaging of the body and its tissues.
- [**CT (computed tomography) scans**](/en/ct) – fast, three-dimensional images of internal organs and structures.
- [**X-ray diagnostics**](/en/x-ray) – exams of bones and other body parts in a range of areas.
- [**Mammography**](/en/mammography) – low-dose breast screening for the early detection of breast cancer.

## Other services

- **Comprehensive screening**, for example breast screening with 3D mammography and ultrasound.
- [**Online and phone booking**](/en/contact#buda) – for MRI, CT, X-ray and screening exams.
- [**SOS and weekend hotline**](/en/sos-hotline) – fast exam scheduling in urgent cases, even on weekends.

## About us

Caring Medical Buda puts the emphasis on the quality of its diagnostic technology and on the patient experience:

- Modern MRI and CT equipment
- **MRI under anesthesia** – with close anesthesiology support, for patients who need it
- A network of several diagnostic centers, including **a site in Budaörs** with modern services
