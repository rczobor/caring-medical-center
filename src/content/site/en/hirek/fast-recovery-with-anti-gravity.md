"Faster recovery and professional rehabilitation, combined with the latest technology – this is what Medical Point offers with its newest weight-supported, anti-gravity treadmill, the "Alter G Fit" 100."

Run by Medical Point in Újpest under expert supervision, this special treadmill gives huge help and a faster path to recovery both to active athletes and to patients in rehabilitation after a joint injury or orthopedic surgery.

Today Medical Point is a trusted source of medical support in Hungary for professional athletes and for patients dealing with musculoskeletal injuries or recovering from surgery. For four years now, it has also been one of the best-known diagnostic and rehabilitation centers for Hungarian sports clubs, and their official medical partner. Founded and led by radiographer and diagnostician Szekér Zoltán, the private medical unit recently added to its rehabilitation and physical therapy equipment: the Alter G Fit 100 anti-gravity treadmill, a weight-supported treadmill that can take off up to 80% of your body weight.

Examples from Hungary and abroad show that athletes who do their rehabilitation or physical therapy on Alter G weight-supported treadmills often reach a full, stable recovery weeks or months sooner than competitors recovering from similar injuries without this option. The same is true for private individuals who want to get back to everyday life as soon as possible, without long breaks from work and family life.

Whether the goal is to keep playing sport professionally or to return to everyday life as soon as possible, this device helps a great deal: besides improving the quality of rehabilitation, it gains you something nothing can replace – a significant amount of time.

It can be especially important for keeping up fitness and muscle condition in people who, because of their age, can no longer do intense exercise, since it would put heavy strain on their joints and might cause pain. Thanks to its weight support, the Alter G Fit 100 can be used very early, even within weeks after surgery. This greatly helps prevent muscle loss, and the load on the joints stays fully under control.

The system's biggest advantage is that the Alter G can measure every important data point in great detail, thanks to its built-in real-time monitoring system. It is also highly personalized technology: using neoprene shorts in different sizes, the treadmill fits your body precisely and controls your movement and how much weight is taken off.

Medical Point's trained physiotherapists welcome anyone who would like to try the treadmill or use it regularly, as well as patients and athletes who choose the anti-gravity treadmill as their path to a fast recovery.
