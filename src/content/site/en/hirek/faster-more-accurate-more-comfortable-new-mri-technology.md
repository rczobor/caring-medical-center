At Medical Point, we keep working to give everyone who comes to us the best possible diagnostic care. Now we are introducing an upgrade that not only raises our professional quality even further, but also makes exams much more comfortable for patients: GE Healthcare's new, AI-based Air Recon DL technology.

## What does this mean in practice?

The new technology needs major hardware and software upgrades. So in mid-August we paused for a short technical break, and then launched the new system on Saturday, August 23.

Excellent MRI quality takes enough time: a spine or knee scan could take as long as 17–20 minutes. Thanks to Air Recon DL, the same scan now takes 8–12 minutes. Less exam time means less discomfort and a faster diagnosis.

And it is not only the exam time that gets shorter. With AI-supported image reconstruction, the images are much clearer, sharper and free of noise. This means the radiologists see the details better: they can more easily spot an early herniated disc, a torn ligament or even small changes in the cartilage.

For musculoskeletal problems, this is a huge advantage. An accurate diagnosis is the first step to recovery: the sooner and the more surely we find out what is causing the pain, the sooner the right treatment can start – whether that is physiotherapy, injection therapy, magnetic or laser therapy, or surgery.

## What does the upgrade offer professional footballers?

When a professional footballer came to us with a knee injury, the MRI scan used to take up to half an hour. Now, thanks to Air Recon DL, the scan is done in just 10 minutes. On the sharp, detailed images, the specialist will see exactly whether it is a torn cruciate ligament or a cartilage or meniscus injury. This means the player gets a diagnosis faster and can start rehabilitation sooner – and so spends less time off the pitch. Sometimes even 20 minutes makes a big difference…

## Why will it be better for everyone?

Or if, for example, a long-distance trail runner comes to us with lasting pain, a spine MRI made with the new technology will show differences between the discs much more clearly than before. This makes it easier for our radiologist to tell whether the problem is caused by an early herniation, nerve root irritation or another degenerative change. Based on the result, a targeted treatment plan is made, which helps the athlete return to running safely and sooner.

## Time and quality together

So with Air Recon DL, we gain both time and quality. Our patients can expect shorter exams, and specialists can make their decisions based on even more accurate images. We believe everyone benefits: the exam is more comfortable, and the care is more reliable.

At Medical Point, it matters to us to always offer the most advanced technology – in diagnostics too. Air Recon DL is not just a new upgrade: it brings a real change to how the exam works and to its quality.
