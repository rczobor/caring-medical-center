Many of you know that one of my/our big goals is to make up for every shortcoming at the clubs, so that the kids can truly get the most out of their careers!

Making up for these shortcomings includes making the right diagnosis (as soon as possible) after a young athlete is injured, and providing quality rehabilitation for the injury. This way, the kids can return to their clubs at the right time and in the best possible condition.

Over the past year and a half, we have (also) seen many athletes who were injured during club training.

However, more than one of these young athletes was sadly injured again because of an inaccurate diagnosis made by the club and the wrong rehabilitation that followed from it. So a recovery could take double or triple the time; in other words, instead of a few weeks, even getting an accurate diagnosis could drag on for months.

From today, every young athlete on the Lazar Project team can get an MRI or ultrasound scan within as little as 4 hours of an injury, along with a specialist's assessment (even on weekends). From now on, we provide you with every step between the injury and the return to the pitch, “in the right order”:

diagnosis and specialist opinion, physiotherapist/rehabilitation coach, treatments (laser, ultrasound, shockwave therapy, magnetic therapy), mental coach, strength and conditioning coach.

This way, the kids are guaranteed to return to training in the shortest possible time and in the best condition. From now on, it can no longer happen that a child later fails to live up to the hopes placed in them because the professionals at their club handled things wrongly before.

(Once again, all respect to the exceptions!)

P.S.

Our partner also provided urgent diagnostic imaging for the matches of the 2021 European Football Championship played in Hungary, so Dembélé, the French national team footballer who was injured, also visited them recently.

Thank you for the cooperation!

“Everything for the next generation”
