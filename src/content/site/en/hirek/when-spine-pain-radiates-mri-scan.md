Common complaints alongside spine pain:

- Numbness in the arm
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Ringing in the ears (tinnitus)
- Numbness in the lower back
- Pain radiating into the leg or thigh

These are typical problems whose cause we cannot find without proper diagnostics. It is important to rule out organic causes, or to confirm what is behind the numbness. A quick, painless [MRI scan](https://medicalpoint.hu/service/mri-vizsgalat/) helps with this.

Investigating spine pain

In our experience, most of our clients try rest and taking it easy before they come to us with spine pain. But in these conditions, a cervical, thoracic or lumbar spine MRI scan can give clinicians – and in many cases physiotherapists too – information precise enough to plan the best treatment.
