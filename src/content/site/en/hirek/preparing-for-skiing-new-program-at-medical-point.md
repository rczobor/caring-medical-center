Winter sports, skiing and snowboarding, offer a fantastic chance to be active outdoors, but unfortunately they also carry a risk of injury. Medical Point Health Center is committed to helping its clients prepare for winter sports and so reduce the chance of injury.

## Why is it important to prepare for skiing?

Skiing puts a heavy load on the body, especially the legs. Without proper physical preparation, the risk of injuries such as torn ligaments, muscle strains and other musculoskeletal problems increases. A training program beforehand can help strengthen your muscles, improve balance and coordination, and build endurance, reducing the chance of injury.

## What is Medical Point's ski preparation program?

At Medical Point, we have developed a special program that specifically builds the skills you need for skiing. The program includes:

- 6 sessions of specialized training: This training includes sport-specific proprioceptive exercises, muscle-strengthening and endurance-building exercises.
- 3 sessions of SIS (Super Inductive System) magnetic therapy: This treatment helps speed up recovery, reduces pain and improves the condition of the muscles.

## Benefits of the program

- Stronger muscles: Strong muscles give more protection against injury.
- Better balance and coordination: Better balance and coordination help you avoid falls.
- More endurance: With better endurance, you can enjoy skiing for longer without getting tired.
- Faster recovery: The SIS treatments help you recover faster after training.

## Who do we recommend the program for?

We recommend the program for anyone who wants to ski, whether a beginner or an advanced skier. It is especially recommended for people returning to skiing after a long break, or who have been injured while skiing before.
