_On July 20 and 24, 2023, we are holding a Stroke Neuro Rehab Open Day at Medical Point Health Center._

Stroke Neuro Rehab is an institute for rehabilitation after neurological injury, mainly for stroke survivors and for anyone who has had any kind of brain event or neurological condition. In our experience, it can also be applied very well and effectively for people with multiple sclerosis and Parkinson's disease.

SNR® is an intensive, active, functional rehabilitation program. With the SNR®/ARNI method (ARNI – Action For Rehabilitation From Neurological Injury), participants can overcome the effects of brain injury while getting guidance for an active everyday life.

The ARNI method is a training approach developed for stroke survivors and people with brain injuries, based on clinical and university research, functional training and individual coping strategies. It is a personalized training program that ensures individual progress in activities and encourages the person to do exercises on their own at home.

## The four main elements of the ARNI method

- Exercise to improve circulation
- Task-oriented exercises
- Specific strength training
- Physical, coping or self-management strategies.

Survivors need an evidence-based, innovative and personalized training strategy, low-cost aids and professional support to help them return to everyday, active life.

ARNI training is exactly that. We also involve family members, because the techniques and exercises learned have to be built into daily life regularly. Activities such as getting dressed soon become pain-free and easier, which is a huge source of joy.

## The ARNI motto

Use it or lose it! Where there's a will, there's a way! Use it and improve it! What we practice will get better. We have to practice what we want to improve!
