## We have opened our third diagnostic center, in Budaörs

Caring Medical Center continues to expand its healthcare services: after Caring Medical Point and Caring Medical Buda, our network's third diagnostic center, Caring Medical Budaörs, has opened its doors.

Our new center in Budaörs, at Puskás Tivadar út 8, will welcome patients in a modern setting, with a high standard of medical expertise behind it.

## Services available at Caring Medical Budaörs

- MRI scans
- ultrasound diagnostics
- laboratory tests
- physical therapy treatments

The aim of this expansion is for the Caring Medical Center network to provide fast, accurate and reliable diagnostic solutions to patients more widely and more accessibly.

Caring Medical Center is committed to contributing to effective treatment and to protecting patients' health with up-to-date equipment, experienced professionals and a patient-centered approach.

## Caring Medical Budaörs – fast, precise MRI diagnostics when time matters

Caring Medical Budaörs represents a new level of modern diagnostics: extra-fast MRI scans, full artificial intelligence (AI) support, and a technological background that is currently unique in Hungary.

At our center, we work with the Siemens Magnetom Flow Plus MRI scanner – a latest-generation, high-performance system that allows imaging up to 40% faster than conventional MRI scans, while image quality stays exceptionally detailed. This is especially useful for joint, spine, head, orbit and breast MRI scans.

## Fast scan – fast report – confident decision

One of the greatest strengths of Caring Medical Budaörs is speed and predictability. For neuroradiology exams (head, spine, orbit), the radiology report is ready the same day, so patients and their treating doctors get reliable information without delay.

## After the exam, you can have

- a report consultation led by a radiologist,
- and, with the radiology report in hand, a specialist consultation arranged for you (orthopedics, neurology, neurosurgery, gynecology, breast diagnostics).

## AI-supported diagnostics – in the service of accuracy

At our center, the diagnostic process is supported by AI-based analysis systems. They assist the radiologist and contribute to even more accurate, structured and reproducible reporting. AI does not replace the doctor – it reinforces the decision.

## Why do doctors recommend Caring Medical Budaörs?

- Extra-fast MRI scans
- Unique Siemens Magnetom Flow Plus technology
- A fully AI-supported diagnostic background
- Same-day report for neuroradiology exams
- Radiology report consultation and referral to specialists
- A calm, patient-centered environment

Caring Medical Budaörs is made for patients who don't want to wait, and for doctors who expect a fast, accurate and reliable diagnosis.
