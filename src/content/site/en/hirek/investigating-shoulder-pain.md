Shoulder pain, frozen shoulder, labral injuries in athletes. Perhaps the most uncomfortable kinds of pain.

Our clients mostly come to us after a moderate blow, a fall or a minor accident, so that we can find the cause of shoulder pain that won't ease. While people usually contact us for knee and spine exams before seeing a specialist, with shoulder pain clients more typically come to us after a specialist visit.

Behind shoulder pain

Cartilage thickness, the joint surface, changes in the rotator cuff that surrounds the shoulder joint or a possible tear, and the condition of the soft tissues can all be assessed very well with an [MRI scan](https://medicalpoint.hu/service/vall-izulet-mr/). This gives the orthopedic specialist an accurate picture of whether rest, physiotherapy or possibly surgery can bring lasting relief from your shoulder pain.
