### An injury is not just pain: it is also a biological crisis

When a muscle is injured (e.g. a strain or a tear), the body immediately starts **regeneration processes**. This is a **very delicate biological balance** involving several factors:

- **Protein breakdown and resynthesis**: damaged muscle fibers are broken down, then rebuilt with new muscle proteins.
- **Inflammatory hormones (cytokines, prostaglandins)** are activated – they regulate cell formation, but **too much of them can hinder healing**.
- **Myoblasts (muscle stem cells)** are activated, but they can only work effectively with enough oxygen and good microcirculation.

## What happens if the injury is not treated properly?

Many people think rest is enough. In fact:

- **Regeneration takes too long**, and the muscle loses its original structure.
- **Scarring develops** – the damaged muscle fibers are replaced by **connective tissue**, which is neither elastic nor strong.
- This scar tissue **becomes a "weak link"**, with a risk of re-injury.
- Functional movement becomes restricted, and returning to sport **becomes risky**.
