A new generation of knee and prostate MRI scans is now available at Caring Medical Center, adding an AI-based analysis system to modern radiological diagnostics.

The AI-based diagnostic system supports the radiologist's work with:

- structured anatomical analysis,
- automatic measurements,
- standardized assessment,
- and preliminary marking of certain abnormalities.

The aim of the technology is to support even more accurate, consistent and higher-quality MRI diagnostics, while the final radiology report is always written and signed off by a radiologist.

Caring Medical Center's goals:

- to increase diagnostic accuracy,
- to make assessment more consistent,
- and to support modern, high-quality patient care.

Currently available:

- Knee MRI with AI support
- Prostate MRI with AI support

Before or after your knee or prostate MRI scan, our MRI radiographers will be happy to tell you how the AI-assisted diagnostic system works.
