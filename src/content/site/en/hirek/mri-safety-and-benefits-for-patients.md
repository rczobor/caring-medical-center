The introduction of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) brought a major step forward in medical diagnosis and treatment. However, the complexity of the procedure and the technology it uses raise many questions about safety and the patient experience. In this section, we explore the safety of MRI scans and the benefits patients gain from them.

## Safety in MRI

1. No ionizing radiation: One of the biggest advantages of MRI is that, unlike X-ray or CT, it does not use ionizing radiation. This avoids radiation exposure and its risks.

2. Safety of contrast agents: Although some MRI scans are done with a contrast agent, most MRI-specific contrast agents are well tolerated and rarely cause side effects. Even so, there is a risk of allergic reactions, and caution is needed in patients with impaired kidney function.

3. Magnetic field: The strong magnetic field used for MRI scans is generally considered safe for the human body. However, before the scan it is important to rule out any metal objects or electronic devices, as these can be dangerous in the magnetic field.

4. Noise: During an MRI scan you may hear a lot of noise, which can be unpleasant. Patients are often given earplugs or headphones to reduce the discomfort.

## Benefits for patients

1. Non-invasive procedure: MRI is a non-invasive imaging method, which means no surgery is needed and nothing has to be inserted into the body.

2. Detailed imaging: MRI makes it possible to examine soft tissues, such as joints, muscles or the brain, in detail. In many cases this imaging provides important information for the diagnosis.

3. Dynamic exams: MRI also allows functional and dynamic exams, such as measuring blood flow or tracking brain activity.

4. Customizable: The scan can be adjusted to the patient's specific needs to get the best possible image quality.

5. Environmentally friendly: MRI scans do not require radioactive materials or produce hazardous waste, so they have a smaller environmental impact.

MRI scans are one of the safest and most informative methods among modern diagnostic procedures. As the technology develops, these benefits will only grow: patients will gain even more, while healthcare professionals get more accurate and reliable information for diagnosing and treating diseases and disorders. MRI diagnostics is a huge step forward in improving the quality and efficiency of patient care.
