Before we opened our health center in the summer of 2017, we thought a lot about what would make us truly useful to our clients with musculoskeletal problems.

The recipe seemed simple:

“The most modern diagnostic imaging, with fast reporting, connections to specialists for any surgery that may be needed, and a specialist clinic for quick advice. Added to this, the widest possible range of advanced musculoskeletal therapy: massage therapy and physiotherapy, physical therapy, laser therapy, shockwave therapy and the most modern magnetic therapy – all of these help us put together the best treatment program for our clients.”

After the fastest possible diagnosis, if surgery can be avoided, we put together an individual protocol for every client. The program usually runs in half-hour blocks, for up to several weeks, and depends on the specific condition, the level of pain and the goal to be reached. If the goal is to restore performance as soon as possible and to get rid of the pain or problem almost immediately, we have gained considerable experience in this too with our amateur and elite athlete clients.
