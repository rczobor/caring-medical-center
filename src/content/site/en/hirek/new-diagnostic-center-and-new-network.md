For more than a decade, [radiographer Szekér Zoltán](https://szekerzoltan.com/) has deliberately built MRI diagnostic centers whose work centers on diagnostic quality that supports clinical decisions. The two Budapest centers he set up earlier – with different professional focuses but the same approach – have become a stable point of reference for referring doctors. With the opening of the third center in January 2026, this way of working has grown into a network. The launch of **Caring Medical Budaörs** created the Caring Medical Center network: three complementary centers with shared professional principles, working in coordination.

The network's purpose is clear: to reduce diagnostic uncertainty. In clinical practice, it is increasingly common for symptoms not to fit a clear-cut condition, while decisions are still urgent. In these situations, what is needed is a targeted, interpretable diagnostic answer tailored to the clinical question, and the right support along the patient pathway. Caring Medical Center offers an organized, structured answer to this challenge. Instead of the number of exams, it puts diagnostic accuracy, relevance and usefulness first: the right exam, in the right place, in the right professional setting.

## A network, not a chain

The Caring Medical Center network deliberately breaks with the standardized, "the same everywhere" service model. Here, working as a network means giving each center its own professional focus. Each center is shaped around different clinical needs, while all of them work by shared professional principles. These principles rest on targeted MRI protocols, structured reporting and consistent radiological thinking. The clinical question always comes before the technical solution. An exam is the answer to a specific problem. This approach is what lets the report be read as part of the patient pathway.

The advantage of the network is that each patient is directed, on professional grounds, to the diagnostic setting that best fits their clinical question. This reduces unnecessary exams, shortens the patient pathway and makes decisions safer.

## The role of the Budaörs center

Caring Medical Budaörs, which opened in 2026, is the newest member of the network, designed specifically to support high-volume, everyday clinical decisions. One of the key aims in designing the center was to pair high-end technology with stable, predictable operation. The center's newly developed Siemens MAGNETOM Flow 1.5T MRI system, the first to be installed in Hungary, delivers reliable, top-of-the-range image quality, while optimized exam times improve patient comfort and also make exams easier to complete. This matters especially for patients who are in pain, have limited mobility or feel anxious, where the success of the exam directly affects image quality and so its diagnostic value.

_“One key element of the Budaörs model is a predictable patient pathway. For our clients, diagnostics here is a transparent process: plannable appointments, uniform protocols, consistent reporting and clear communication. The goal is for the MRI scan to be a logical step in treatment. Beyond quality and speed, the real value of diagnostics is measured in how safely decisions can be made”_ _– said Szekér Zoltán, who owns and runs the centers._

## Decision support at every level

The essence of the Caring Medical Center network is that the radiology report should not stand on its own. Structured wording, a clear answer to the clinical question and comparability all serve one purpose: making diagnostics a real basis for the next step – whether that is a decision by your family doctor (GP), a specialist consultation or choosing a course of treatment.

This approach is what unites the network: different locations, different focuses, but the same quality principles. Within this system, Caring Medical Budaörs is a diagnostic hub that provides a stable, reliable basis for everyday clinical decisions.
