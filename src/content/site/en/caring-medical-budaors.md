**Caring Medical Budaörs** is the **new diagnostic center** of the **Caring Medical Center** network. It opened its doors in Budaörs in 2026 to extend the services and accessibility of the existing Budapest and Buda sites. The center aims to give patients and their doctors fast, accurate diagnostic answers tailored to clinical questions, in a modern setting with high professional standards.

Services

Caring Medical Budaörs mainly carries out diagnostic exams, including:

Imaging diagnostics

- [MRI diagnostics – radiation-free, detailed imaging of the body and its tissues.](/en/mri)
- [Ultrasound diagnostics](/en/ultrasound)
- [Laboratory tests](https://whitelab.hu/idopontfoglalas)
- [Physical therapy](/en/rehabilitation/physiotherapy)

Other services

- Comprehensive screening
- [Online and phone booking](/en/contact#budaors)
- [SOS and weekend hotline](/en/sos-hotline) – fast exam scheduling in urgent cases, even on weekends.

About us

Caring Medical Budaörs puts the emphasis on the quality of its diagnostic technology and on the patient experience:

- Modern MRI equipment
- Fast reporting, within as little as 1 working day in urgent cases
- A network of several diagnostic centers, including a site in Budaörs with modern services
