Cardiovascular disease is the most common cause of death. That is why an annual cardiology check-up is definitely recommended: as a preventive step, it can find possible problems before they become chronic.

## Which symptoms mean you should definitely visit the clinic?

- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain
- Strong, pounding heartbeat
- Dizziness or weakness
- Blood pressure problems
- Unexplained sudden numbness in the arm

## Recommended tests available at our clinic:

- Cardiac ultrasound (echocardiography)
- Cardiac MRI
