Traumatology (also called trauma surgery) is a specialty dedicated to treating unexpected injuries caused by accidents. Our joint service treats simpler injuries that are not life-threatening, such as sports injuries, ski accidents, or accidents at home, at school or at work: e.g. fractures, dislocations, bruises, sprains, pulled muscles, etc.

## Urgent injury care and urgent MRI scans

Medical Point and its partner institution, [Emineo Private Hospital](https://emineo.hu/), offer their patients a service that is unique in the country. Thanks to this special cooperation, if you are injured in an accident, you receive an accurate diagnosis and expert care in the shortest possible time.

This combined injury care service is available every day of the week, including public holidays, from 08:00 to 20:00.

## Even with an accident injury, it is important that you are first seen by a traumatology specialist, who can guide the radiologist on what kind of problem to look for during the MRI scan. So if you have an accident or injury, please be sure to call [06 1 773 3333](tel:+3617733333) or book an appointment online:

If the nature of the injury calls for it, the traumatology specialist will recommend an MRI scan, which you can have with us within one hour. The scan is evaluated urgently within one hour, so the doctor who requested it and you receive the diagnosis in the shortest possible time, and the treatment of your injury can be completed.

## Why our service is special:

- for minor injuries, you don't have to wait for hours: we can provide care quickly
- if a digital X-ray is not enough for your care, we arrange your MRI scan extra fast as well – **within 2 hours at most**
- but an MRI scan is worth nothing if no one is there to evaluate it, so we have also arranged urgent reporting by a radiologist: **within 1 hour** on weekdays, and at weekends **on the day of the scan, or within 48 hours at the latest**!
- our service package is worth even more because, if the injury needs surgery, traumatology and orthopedic specialists are available at our partner institution, together with high-quality operating room and inpatient facilities.

In everyday life, and even more so in injury care, TIME is one of the most important things.
