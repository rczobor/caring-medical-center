Rheumatology deals mainly with inflammatory and wear-related diseases of the spine, and with treating structures outside the joints, such as ligaments, tendons and muscles.

## Which conditions does rheumatology treat?

- Joint inflammation and pain not caused by an injury (ankle, knee, hip, elbow, shoulder)
- Joint wear (cracking joints)
- Spine and back pain
- Suspected osteoporosis
