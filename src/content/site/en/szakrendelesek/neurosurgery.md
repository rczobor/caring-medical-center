Neurosurgery/neurology is the branch of medicine that deals with diseases of the spine and the nervous system. After a thorough examination, the specialist often treats these problems with surgery.

Nervous system problems can also be linked to damage to other organs. In these cases, specialists try to provide comprehensive therapy by working together with other specialists and with physiotherapists. A range of invasive and non-invasive methods help reach an accurate diagnosis.

After an accurate diagnosis – which may include MRI or CT imaging – the neurosurgeon performs surgery on the brain and spine when needed.

At Medical Point, patients mainly have a physical examination. When needed, we can do an MRI scan on site.

## When should you see a neurosurgeon?

Headache, loss of consciousness, dizziness, sleep problems, visual disturbances, memory loss, numbness. The treatment of epilepsy and Parkinson's disease also belongs to neurosurgery.

## The neurosurgeon specializes in treating the following problems

- Herniated disc
- Slipped vertebra (spondylolisthesis) or vertebral injury
- Spinal stenosis
- Pinched nerve
- Trigeminal neuralgia
- Spinal tumors / brain tumors
- Unexplained sudden numbness in the arm

## Which therapies can help recovery?

## Important: treatments can only be given after an individual assessment suited to the type of condition!

- injection treatments: painkillers and anti-inflammatory medicines
- physiotherapy: exercise therapy, manual therapy, massage therapy
- physical therapy: shockwave therapy, SIS magnetic therapy, laser therapy
- surgery: arranging surgery when needed
