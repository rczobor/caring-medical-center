Naturopathy, phytotherapy and integrative nutrition counseling at the Medical Point Health Center. In naturopathic counseling, we look at how your body works as a whole – on both a physical and an emotional level. The goal is not just to relieve symptoms, but to restore balance through the body's own healing processes.

At the heart of the consultation is phytotherapy: the targeted, personalized use of active plant compounds, which gently yet effectively support the body's regeneration. We always put together our recommendations based on your individual condition, your life situation and the signals your body sends.

## During counseling, we pay particular attention to:

## • supporting the nervous system • restoring hormonal balance • strengthening the immune system • and relieving physical and emotional symptoms caused by stress

## As part of the consultation, an integrative, functional dietary approach is also available. This does not mean writing a classic diet plan; instead, it helps you look at:

- which foods support your body and which put a strain on it,
- where you can gently create balance in your eating,
- and how nutrition can help support hormonal function, digestion and the balance of the nervous system.

I don't think in calories or lists of forbidden foods. I think about understanding what your body needs, and how nutrition can also become a source of support on the path to physical and emotional regeneration.

The counseling does not replace medical treatment. It plays a complementary, harmonizing role, taking into account your existing diagnoses, treatments and individual health background.
