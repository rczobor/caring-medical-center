Hand surgery covers the diagnosis and treatment of conditions of the hand, wrist and forearm – from accident injuries to overuse and wear-and-tear conditions.

## Our most common areas of care

## Nerve compression syndromes

These include carpal tunnel, cubital tunnel and Guyon's canal syndrome. These conditions result from pressure on the nerves, which can cause numbness, pain or weakness. Early diagnosis and targeted treatment help avoid permanent nerve damage.

## Tendon injuries and tendon reconstruction

Flexor and extensor tendon injuries are often caused by an accident, but they can also result from chronic overuse. We restore the hand's full range of motion with modern microsurgical techniques and a rehabilitation program.

## Hand fractures and bone fractures

We treat fractures of the hand and fingers with surgery when needed, and conservatively in other cases. The aim is precise anatomical restoration, so the hand fully regains its function.

## Ligament injuries, instability

Instability of the wrist or hand is often the result of a poorly healed injury. We identify the problem with specialized diagnostic methods (MRI, ultrasound) and, when needed, treat it with ligament reconstruction.

## Degenerative joint diseases

Osteoarthritis of the hand and thumb (CMC I) is a common complaint, especially in older patients or patients who do manual work. Treatment options range from conservative therapy to joint replacement.

## Reconstructive and restorative surgery

We correct scars, deformities and restricted movement after accidents with precise hand surgery techniques. Besides restoring function, the aim is also a good cosmetic result.

## Tumors, ganglions, cysts

Lumps on the hand (e.g. ganglion, lipoma, cyst) are removed with a quick, safe and, in most cases, minimally invasive procedure.

## Hand nerve injuries

Reconstruction after nerve injuries is key to preserving the hand's sensory and motor functions. Microsurgery and post-operative rehabilitation together give the best result.

## Pediatric hand surgery

Recognizing congenital hand conditions and growth disorders early is decisive for successful restoration. For children, we use gentle techniques that respect their development.

## Our services

## Hand surgery consultation with a specialist

We establish the exact diagnosis with a detailed physical examination and imaging, then explain your treatment options – whether conservative or surgical.

## Second opinion

An independent expert review of previous diagnoses, reports and surgical recommendations, so you can make an informed decision about further treatment.

## Imaging

- **X-ray:** fast, accurate information about changes in bones and joints.
- **MRI:** a detailed picture of tendons, ligaments, cartilage and nerves.
- **Ultrasound:** a dynamic exam during movement, without pain.

## Injection treatments

Anti-inflammatory or hyaluronic acid injections help treat joint and tendon sheath pain, such as tennis elbow. These procedures quickly reduce symptoms and support regeneration.

## Splints and orthoses

We provide custom splints and braces that stabilize the affected area and help it heal faster.

## Surgical care

- Surgical repair of hand and finger fractures
- Microsurgical reconstruction of tendon and nerve injuries
- Joint replacement for osteoarthritis
- Minimally invasive procedures: e.g. **tunnel syndrome surgery**, **ganglion removal**

## Rehabilitation and physiotherapy

After surgical or conservative treatment, rehabilitation is key. Our physiotherapists and manual therapists help restore function with a personalized program.

- **Physiotherapy:** regaining range of motion and muscle strength
- **Manual therapy:** joint mobilization, restoring muscle balance
- **Complementary treatments:** ultrasound, laser therapy, TENS, BEMER – to support circulation and regeneration
