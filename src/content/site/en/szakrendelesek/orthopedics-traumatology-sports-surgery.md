Orthopedics is the specialty that deals with acquired and congenital disorders of the supporting (bone) and moving (muscle) systems, and with their treatment. It covers in particular the treatment of disorders of the limbs, of the bones and muscles, and of how the spine works.

## What kinds of examinations belong to orthopedics?

- Examination of the large joints (shoulder, hip, knee, ankle) for degenerative changes and deep tendon reflexes
- Gait assessment (e.g. flat feet)
- Posture assessment
- Functional examination of the chest and spine
- Spine and knee problems
- Hip problems
- Diseases of the limb bones

## Sports surgery (sports traumatology)

Sports surgery is a specialty on the border of orthopedics and traumatology. It deals with injuries and chronic degenerative changes that occur in connection with sport or sport-like activity, and today it works essentially as an independent discipline. It deals mainly with joint and muscle injuries and disorders.
