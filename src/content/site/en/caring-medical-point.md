Caring Medical Point – Clinic overview

**Caring Medical Point Health Center** and **Caring** **Medical Point MRI Diagnostics** is a private **diagnostic, rehabilitation and specialist outpatient center** in Budapest that offers a full range of imaging exams, specialist clinics and alternative/rehabilitation services, all in one place.

**Address:** [1138 Budapest, Madarász Viktor u. 47-49.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Budapest,+Madar%C3%A1sz+Viktor+u.+47-49,+1138/@47.5541135,19.0768973,738m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x4741dbccc2955b37:0x868c1eafaa71800c!8m2!3d47.5541099!4d19.0794722!16s%2Fg%2F11rhq6nq2b?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDEyOC4wIKXMDSoKLDEwMDc5MjA2OUgBUAM%3D) **Phone:** [+36 1 800 9771](tel:+3618009771) / [+36 (70) 427-1127](tel:+36704271127) (MRI information)

Clinical profile

Diagnostic services

- [**MRI scans**](/en/mri) – MRI exams without a referral, available even on weekends.
- [**Digital X-ray**](/en/x-ray) – modern, patient-friendly technology.
- [**Laboratory tests**](/en/laboratory-tests) – blood, urine and hormone tests for routine checks and targeted diagnostics.
- [**Ultrasound**](/en/ultrasound) – fast, painless exams of internal organs, joints and blood vessels.

Specialist clinics

Caring Medical Point offers several kinds of specialist care, often followed or preceded by a larger diagnostic exam:

- [**Urgent care**](/en/specialist-clinics/urgent-care)
- [**Neurosurgery**](/en/specialist-clinics/neurosurgery)
- [**Traumatology and sports surgery**](/en/specialist-clinics/orthopedics-traumatology-sports-surgery)
- [**Rheumatology**](/en/specialist-clinics/rheumatology)
- [**Cardiology and sports cardiology**](/en/specialist-clinics/cardiology)
- [**Neurology (telemedicine)**](/en/specialist-clinics/neurology)
- [**Hand surgery**](/en/specialist-clinics/hand-surgery)
- [**Orthopedics**](/en/specialist-clinics/orthopedics-traumatology-sports-surgery)
- [**Spine care**](/en/spine-care)

Rehabilitation and alternative therapies

Caring Medical Point offers not only diagnostics but also targeted rehabilitation and complementary therapy services:

- [**Physiotherapy and physical therapy**](/en/rehabilitation/physiotherapy)
- [**Massage therapy**](/en/rehabilitation/massage-therapy)
- [**Shockwave therapy**](/en/rehabilitation/shockwave-therapy)
- [**Kinesiology taping**](/en/rehabilitation/kinesiology-taping)
- [**Laser therapy**](/en/rehabilitation/laser-therapy)
- [**Magnetic therapy (including mobile treatment at home, as the service is rolled out)**](/en/rehabilitation/magnetic-therapy)

This comprehensive approach aims not only to make the diagnosis but also to provide full rehabilitation and restore movement.

Staff and expertise

The clinic works with a well-prepared medical and diagnostic team experienced in several fields. Its leadership includes specialists who focus on MRI diagnostics and musculoskeletal care.
