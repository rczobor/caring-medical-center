## When is a lab test recommended?

- Complete blood count to analyze your blood cells and blood composition
- Urine test to check how your kidneys and urinary tract are working
- Hormone level analysis to check thyroid and sex hormone function
- Food intolerance tests to detect allergic reactions
- Tumor marker tests for early signs of cancer
- Vitamin and mineral levels to check your body's nutrient status

## What should you know before a lab test?

Before certain lab tests, such as a blood sugar test, you need to arrive on an empty stomach. Otherwise you can eat and drink before the test and take your usual medication.

We give every patient the exact details and any specific instructions for their lab tests in advance.

## How long does a lab test take and how does it work?

The test itself usually takes a few minutes, and the sample is taken quickly and painlessly. Results are ready within 1–3 days, and we email them to you.

After evaluating the results, our specialists recommend further tests or treatment if needed.
