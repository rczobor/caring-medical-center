## Mental Performance Program

Performance starts in the mind.

Physical preparation lays the foundation for performance. In the decisive moments, focus, confidence, managing emotions and making quick decisions help athletes bring out everything they have worked for over months or years.

## Body and mind working together

Athletic performance is shaped by fitness, technical skill, tactical preparation and mental state together. At the biggest competitions, small differences often separate a good result from an outstanding one. In these situations, directing your attention, handling pressure and trusting your own abilities matter especially.

The Medical Point Mental Performance Program combines the tools of sport psychology preparation with a sports medicine, rehabilitation and diagnostic approach. Our goal is for athletes to arrive on the field mentally prepared, to handle competition situations more deliberately, and to build their comeback with confidence after an injury.

The work is always tailored to the athlete's goals, age, sport, current situation and competition calendar.

## Personalized support in a multidisciplinary setting

The program's leads coordinate athletes' mental preparation with Medical Point's sports physicians, rehabilitation specialists and therapists.

## Mental preparation at every stage of athletic performance

Different support is needed before a major competition, at a critical moment in a match, during a situation within a team, or after a longer injury.

## Mental preparation before competition

In the period before a competition, we build the mental state that helps athletes access their skills, routines and physical abilities even in the most important moments.

During preparation, athletes learn to manage pre-competition tension deliberately, direct their attention, and develop a personal routine that gives them stability.

### Timing peak form

Deliberate mental preparation for the most important events of the season or competition period.

### Focus under high stakes

Improving concentration, handling distractions and directing attention deliberately.

### Managing pre-competition anxiety

Regulating stage fright, pressure to perform and heightened inner tension.

### Personal competition routine

Developing individual rituals and preparation steps that create a sense of security and predictability.

### Building confidence

Strengthening trust in your own abilities by deliberately drawing on past successes and personal resources.

### Handling unhelpful thoughts

Recognizing the inner dialogue that undermines performance and developing supportive thinking patterns.

## Mental stability during a match

During a match or competition, the situation changes from moment to moment. A mistake, an unexpected turn, an opponent's lead or the crowd's reaction can quickly throw an athlete out of the right mental state.

The aim of preparation is that even in these situations athletes can return to the next task, keep their ability to make decisions, and deliberately regulate their own emotional reactions.

### Immediate stress management

Techniques you can apply quickly in critical or tense situations.

### Focus after mistakes

Letting go of the previous mistake and redirecting attention to the next task.

### Quick decision-making

Confident and accurate choices in fast-paced, complex sporting situations.

### Emotional regulation

Deliberately managing anger, frustration, overexcitement and tension.

### Mental reset

Personalized methods to quickly restore focus and a competitive state of mind.

## Locker-room dynamics and team cohesion

Members of a successful team work toward shared goals, understand their own roles, and can support one another even in tense situations.

Team-level mental development helps bring to the surface the tensions that hold the team back, strengthens shared responsibility, and supports building a consistent team culture.

### Clear communication

Simpler, more direct and more effective communication on the field, in the locker room and in training.

### Shared goals and team culture

Aligning the team's identity, values and performance goals.

### Conflict management

Uncovering internal tensions and building ways of working that support development.

### Supporting leadership roles

Developing the communication and leadership skills of team captains, key players and informal leaders.

### Trust and cooperation

Strengthening relationships, responsibility and mutual support among team members.

Tailored to the team's goals, size and competition calendar.

## Recovery also means rebuilding the athlete's confidence

Beyond physical condition, an injury can affect an athlete's sense of security, motivation and self-image. During rehabilitation, fear of re-injury, uncertainty about performance or a feeling of having lost one's previous level often appear.

Mental support helps you process these experiences, set realistic goals and gradually rebuild trust in your own body.

- Recognizing injury-related mental barriers.
- Reducing the fear of re-injury.
- Rebuilding confidence and a sense of control.
- Mental support for rehabilitation goals.
- Preserving athletic identity during time out.
- Preparing to return to training and competition.

The return is complete when the athlete trusts their body and their own decisions again.

## Deliberate preparation for surgery and rehabilitation

Uncertainty before surgery, fears about the procedure and the expected length of rehabilitation can place a significant mental burden on an athlete. Preparation helps build the inner stability to rely on before surgery and throughout recovery.

### Preparing for surgery

Managing fears and uncertainties about the procedure.

### Setting rehabilitation goals

Defining realistic, measurable and motivating milestones.

### Staying motivated

Mental support through a longer or uneven rehabilitation period.

### Recognizing progress

Noticing small achievements and directing attention to progress.

### Confidence to return

Mental preparation for gradually increasing load and stepping back into sport.

## Ready to Sport

In the Ready to Sport program, mental preparation is a key pillar of a safe return to sport, alongside rehabilitation and medical follow-up.

Physical tests show how the body responds to load. The athlete's mental state shows how much they trust their movement, their decisions and their own performance capacity again.

Rehabilitation achieves its goal when both your body and your mind are ready to return.
