The exam takes only 15–20 minutes and, apart from abdominal and pelvic ultrasound, needs no special preparation. Despite its simplicity, thanks to today's modern equipment and the constant progress of medicine, there are only good reasons to have one or more ultrasound exams not just when you have symptoms, but also every 1–2 years for screening.
