The fee for a radiology second opinion is HUF 30,000, payable once you have sent in your images.

## How does Medreport work?

![How does Medreport work?](@assets/site/ahevK2oLfKA0WreTcJSwkWdmk.png)

- 1
- Send your scan

Send the images of your CT, MRI or X-ray scan through our online system, or bring them to our center. Our call center colleagues can give you full details.

- 2
- Radiology review

Our specialist radiologists prepare the second opinion as quickly as possible.

- Which scans do we give a second opinion on?
- CT scans
- MRI scans
- X-rays
