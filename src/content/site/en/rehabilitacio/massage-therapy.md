Massage therapy is used to identify and treat reversible functional disorders of the musculoskeletal system. The assessment includes examining the reflex responses of the muscles, ligaments and skin, as well as joint mechanics, muscle function and coordination. This helps us locate and analyze movement disorders in the joints of the limbs or in the vertebrae. Based on this analysis, we treat you effectively with a range of hand techniques aimed at relieving pain and restoring mobility.

## Why is it worth doing?

We use hands-on (manual) techniques to release restricted movement in the joints. On the joints, we use traction (pulling), translational gliding and soft tissue techniques. During traction, we move the bones that form the joint apart from each other, which lowers the pressure inside the joint and leads to loosening and pain relief.

The goal is to restore normal joint play, which the various relaxing and stretching soft tissue techniques also help with. By relaxing the muscles as needed, we restore the movement dynamics, function, original alignment and stability of the spine and limb joints. This reduces or eliminates the pain (for example sprains, and neck, back and lower back pain).

As the pain goes away, your movement improves, and at the same time you feel better both mentally and physically.
