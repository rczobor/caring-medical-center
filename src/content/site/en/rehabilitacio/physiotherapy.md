The main goal of physiotherapy is to improve quality of life (better mobility, rehabilitation, less pain), to ease the symptoms of certain conditions (for example osteoporosis, wear-and-tear and other degenerative musculoskeletal conditions) and to prevent further decline. The personalized therapy is always an exercise program tailored to the patient's needs and built around protecting the joints and spine, with both stabilizing and functional exercises.

## Why is it worth doing?

Physiotherapy is a complex therapy that covers everything from prevention through treatment to rehabilitation. With physiotherapy, we can work in a targeted way to preserve your mobility and improve how you move. We use physiotherapy to strengthen and stretch muscles, increase the range of motion of joints, improve coordination and build muscle strength. Our goal is to restore the function of the affected organ, organ system, body part or body region.

We carry out static and dynamic exercises in one-to-one sessions, tailored to your diagnosis and current condition, and we build up gradually. Professional physiotherapy means using special techniques that match the diagnosis while focusing on the whole body.

## What examination do you need before physiotherapy?

If you have not yet seen a doctor and you have ongoing symptoms, we recommend that you first consult a specialist. At our health center you can see specialists in many fields, for example traumatologists, orthopedic surgeons, a neurologist, a neurosurgeon and a rheumatologist.

We also have diagnostic equipment on site, such as MRI, X-ray and ultrasound, which can give you a complete picture of your current condition. After the doctor's diagnosis, our physiotherapists assess your condition, and then you agree together on the course of your recovery.

## We also have physical therapy equipment that complements physiotherapy, such as:

- [B.P.R.S therapy](https://medicalpoint.hu/service/b-p-r-s-terapia/),
- Shockwave therapy and

By combining exercise with these treatments, we can offer our patients complex rehabilitation.
