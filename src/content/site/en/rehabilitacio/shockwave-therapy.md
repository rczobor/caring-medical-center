BTL shockwave therapy is a new, non-invasive solution for bone and muscle pain. Extracorporeal shockwave therapy is widely used in physical therapy, orthopedics and sports medicine. It is mainly used for chronic muscle and tendon sheath conditions, and for back and neck pain. The most common indications are shoulder pain, tennis elbow, lower back pain, Achilles tendinitis, patellar tendon sheath inflammation and trigger points (muscle knots).

## Why is it worth doing?

Extracorporeal acoustic wave therapy (unlike kidney stone lithotripsy) does not destroy tissue. Instead, it triggers interstitial and extracellular biological effects that, among other things, help tissue regenerate. Shockwave treatment significantly increases the presence of growth markers such as eNOS, VEGF, PCNA and BMP. The new blood vessels improve the supply of blood and oxygen, which helps you heal faster. The acoustic waves increase the activity of mast cells, which play a role in resolving chronic inflammation. Other effects: it stimulates collagen production, dissolves calcified fibroblasts and disperses substance P, a pain mediator.
