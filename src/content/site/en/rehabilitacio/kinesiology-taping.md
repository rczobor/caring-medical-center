Kinesio Tape is a therapy that can be used on its own to treat musculoskeletal problems, or alongside other treatments. It supports the affected area or muscle and helps restore how it works.

## What is the treatment done with?

For the treatment, we use a special tape. One side has an adhesive surface, and the other side is a fabric with a special fiber arrangement. The tape is stretchy and elastic. This is very important, because unlike rigid supports, it can support the area dynamically and actively.

## Why is this technique effective?

## The technique is based on constant stimulation of receptors through the skin over several days, which strengthens the body's activating and self-healing processes. As a result, the brain receives a specific stimulus, and the area regenerates faster thanks to stronger and quicker responses. The tape can be applied in several ways, which is why you see such varied patterns. A properly trained professional, taking into account both your specific problem and your individual case, can give you effective help with this special technique!
