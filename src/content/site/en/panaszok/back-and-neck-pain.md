Spine pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal complaints today. It can seriously affect your ability to work, your sleep and your quality of life.

We recommend a specialist consultation if:

- you have lasting lower back, back or neck pain
- the pain spreads into your arm or leg
- you have numbness, muscle weakness or changes in sensation
- you have a known herniated disc or scoliosis
