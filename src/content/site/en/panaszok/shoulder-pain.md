Most people who come to us with shoulder pain do so after a moderate blow, a fall or a minor accident, so that we can find the cause of pain that won't ease. A frozen shoulder is common, and in athletes, so is a labral tear.

The steps below show how you get from exam to treatment with us. If you already have a report, you can also book the specialist clinic directly.
