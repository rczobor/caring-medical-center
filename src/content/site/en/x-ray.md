The digital X-ray used at Caring Medical Point is a patient-friendly device for a new era, and it puts less strain on your body.

X-ray exams can be done at any age, preferably on a specialist's recommendation. During pregnancy and in young children, the exam should be avoided where possible and done only when there is a very strong reason for it. At our clinic, exams are carried out by well-trained specialist assistants.

Radiologists evaluate the images, and we email you the results within 48 hours of the exam.

Our specialists can look at the images straight away and suggest treatment, or refer you for further tests. When taking X-rays, our assistants pay the utmost attention to your radiation protection, using the right protective equipment.

## When is an X-ray recommended?

- Chest X-ray for a long-lasting cough, a pre-operative assessment or a fitness exam
- To examine bones and joints after an injury or for long-lasting pain
- Traumatic injuries (falls, blows, sprains): the first-line exam to identify fractures and dislocations
- Skull X-rays to detect sinusitis and trauma-related changes
- Spine X-rays to assess changes in the vertebrae
- Ear, nose and throat problems: inflammatory changes in the sinuses

## What should you know before an X-ray?

No special preparation is needed: you can eat and drink before the exam and take your medication. Right before the exam, it's important that you have no metal on you or in your clothing.

Some X-ray exams, however, require you to arrive on an empty stomach, for example a kidney X-ray without contrast.

## How long does an X-ray take and how does it work?

It mainly depends on how many images are taken, but it usually takes a few minutes.

An X-ray is an imaging exam that uses ionizing radiation. Some of the X-rays are absorbed by the organs, and the rest pass through the body. Radiation can be harmful to the body, but the important information gained from the images matters far more than the risk of the dose. Of course, the right protective equipment is available to reduce these risks as much as possible.

To keep your radiation exposure low, we look after your health by strictly following professional standards.
